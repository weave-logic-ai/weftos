# [017] VLM-3R: Vision-Language Models Augmented with Instruction-Aligned 3D Reconstruction

**Citation:** Z. Fan, J. Zhang, R. Li, J. Zhang, R. Chen, H. Hu, K. Wang, H. Qu, D. Wang, Z. Yan, et al. *VLM-3r: vision-language models augmented with instruction-aligned 3d reconstruction*. arXiv preprint, 2025.

**arXiv:** [2505.20279](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.20279) · venue not found (arXiv preprint; project page: vlm-3r.github.io)

## Summary
VLM-3R extends 2D-native LMMs to 3D scene understanding by feeding **monocular video** through a built-in **geometry encoder** that produces "implicit 3D tokens" — a learned latent representation of spatial structure, rather than an explicit point cloud or mesh. These 3D tokens are fused with language via a "Spatial-Visual-View Fusion" mechanism and 200K+ curated 3D-reconstructive instruction-tuning QA pairs, so the model can answer spatial/embodied questions directly from raw video without a separate offline reconstruction step. The authors also introduce a Vision-Spatial-Temporal Intelligence benchmark (138.6K+ QA pairs, 5 tasks) targeting evolving spatial relationships over time.

## Method specifics
3D representation: **implicit 3D tokens** from an internal geometry encoder driven by monocular video — no external depth sensor, no explicit point cloud/mesh output exposed to the user, no BEV. This is a **learned latent geometry embedding**, not measured geometry: it is not metric by construction (monocular video → implicit tokens has no built-in scale anchor), and because the tokens are implicit/opaque, there is no exposed AABB or coordinate output that could be checked against a metric reference. Flag: **monocular input, no confirmed metric output** — a textbook case for the WeftOS doctrine that appearance/monocular latents must never be treated as measured geometry.

## Skill-3D relation
Cited in the §2.1 second clause with SpatialRGPT, SpatialVLM, Chat-Scene, Synthetic Vision, Chat-3D, Zhang 2025a as incorporating "3D reconstruction, depth cues, spatial VQA data." VLM-3R's "reconstruction folded into the model as implicit tokens, no external tool call" design is the architectural opposite of Skill-3D's own thesis (external, per-scene tool selection for 3D evidence) — worth noting as a contrasting prior-art design point in the synthesis.

## WeftOS relevance
The implicit-3D-token approach is fundamentally at odds with WeftOS's structure/appearance split (ADR-078): it fuses reconstruction into an opaque latent inside the VLM rather than producing an inspectable, chain-auditable structure like BVH leaves. There is nothing to extract as a `WM_OBJECT`/`WM_SURFACE` leaf — the geometry never becomes explicit. Its temporal/"evolving spatial relationships" benchmark angle is a useful evaluation concept (spatial state over time) that could inspire a future Urth benchmark for tracking object persistence across a MentraOS capture session, but the model itself is not adoptable.

**Verdict: SKIP** — opaque implicit-geometry design conflicts with WeftOS's honest, inspectable-geometry doctrine; only the "spatial-temporal QA benchmark" framing is worth remembering as a possible eval-design idea (WATCH-adjacent, not the model).
